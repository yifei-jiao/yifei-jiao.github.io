# Yifei Jiao | Personal Academic Website

Source repository for [Yifei Jiao's personal academic website](https://yifei-jiao.github.io/).

Yifei Jiao is a postdoctoral researcher in planetary science affiliated with the University of California, Santa Cruz and Tsinghua University. His research focuses on the collisional and dynamical evolution of small planetary bodies, rings, and moons, using theoretical and numerical methods to investigate open questions in the Solar System.

## Website highlights

- Research profile, academic experience, publications, and contact information
- Research on impact dynamics, asteroid collisional evolution, planetary rings, and moons
- Information about open-source SPH simulations for hypervelocity impacts and tidal responses
- A feature page for asteroid **(20765) Yifeijiao**, named in his honor in 2026
- Links to Google Scholar, ORCID, ResearchGate, CV, and other academic resources

## Built with

The site is built with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) and [Hugo Blox](https://hugoblox.com/), and is published through GitHub Pages.
