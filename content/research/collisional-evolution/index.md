---
title: Collisional evolution of asteroids
date: 2025-06-01
summary: How did asteroids evolve due to collisions from cratering to catastrophic disruption?
tags: ["Asteroids"]
---

<span style="font-size:0.85em;">
  
This work investigates how collisions reshape asteroids and what their craters and present-day shapes reveal about their interiors and origins. We used simulations of giant crater formation on 253 Mathilde to constrain its cohesive, porous interior and explore the collisional origins of small carbonaceous asteroids. We also combined SPH–DEM simulations with an artificial neural network to infer plausible impact conditions from basaltic asteroid shapes, identifying a possible sub-catastrophic pathway to Eros-like bilobed remnants. Together, these studies connect material properties, impact physics, and observable morphology to understand asteroid evolution from cratering to catastrophic disruption.

**Y. Jiao**, B. Cheng, W. Dai, et al. <a href="https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-6500771/v1" target="_blank">*Giant craters on 253 Mathilde revealing the cohesive porous interior of carbonaceous parent bodies*.</a> Nature Geoscience (under review)

T. Baoyin, **Y. Jiao**, B. Cheng. <a href="https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/staf1628" target="_blank">*Predicting the collision history of basaltic asteroids from parametrized shapes with an artificial neural network*.</a> Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society (2025)
